# Imply

> Imply is the real-time analytics company founded by the original creators of Apache Druid. Its flagship product, Imply Polaris, is a fully managed database-as-a-service (DBaaS) for building real-time analytics applications, with a REST API to manage tables, files, ingestion jobs, queries, dashboards, and access. Imply also offers Imply Enterprise and Hybrid for self-managed Druid.
